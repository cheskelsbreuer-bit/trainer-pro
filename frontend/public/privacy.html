Privacy Policy

Trainer Pro · Last updated 1 September 2026

Trainer Pro is software used by small childcare and coaching businesses — babysitters, day camps, daycares and personal coaches — to manage their own customers: schedules, attendance, balances and messages. This policy explains what we collect and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Business account information: the business owner's name, email address, and login credentials.
  • Customer records entered by the business: the names of children in their care, a parent or guardian's name, phone number and email address, days attended, allergies and care notes, and the amounts billed and paid.
  • Messages sent between the business and its customers inside the app, and any photos attached to them.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use it

Information is used only to provide the service to the business that entered it: showing them their own schedule and balances, delivering the account notifications they choose to send to their own customers, and processing payments those customers choose to make.

Mobile information and SMS consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

A phone number is used for one purpose only: to deliver the account notifications the customer agreed to receive from the specific business they are enrolled with — balance reminders, payment receipts and schedule announcements. Phone numbers are never sold, rented, or given to advertisers, data brokers, or any other business using Trainer Pro. Consent given to one business is never applied to another.

Providing a mobile number and agreeing to text messages is optional. Declining does not affect a customer's ability to enrol a child, use the service, or make payments.

To stop receiving messages, reply STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP, or contact the business directly. Message and data rates may apply.

Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers needed to run the product — our database and hosting providers, our email delivery provider, our SMS provider, and our payment processor — and only to the extent required to deliver the service. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for advertising or marketing.

How long we keep it

Records are kept while the business's account is active. A business can export or delete its data at any time from within the app. On account closure, data is deleted within 30 days.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access controls that limit each business to its own records. Photos and files are stored privately and served through short-lived signed links.

Children

Records about children are entered by the business that cares for them, under the authority of the child's parent or guardian. Children do not create accounts or interact with Trainer Pro directly.

Your choices

Parents and customers may ask the business they are enrolled with to correct or remove their details at any time, and may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
