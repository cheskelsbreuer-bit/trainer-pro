How parents opt in to text messages

Trainer Pro · Program name: Trainer Pro Account Notifications · Last updated 2 September 2026

Opting in is voluntary and is never required. A parent who does not opt in receives exactly the same childcare service, can still enrol their child, use the parent portal, see their balance and make payments. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of enrolling, of creating an account, of making a payment, or of anything else.

Who sends these messages, and who receives them

Trainer Pro is the sender of record. Trainer Pro is software used by many independent small childcare businesses — babysitters, daycares and day camps — to run their enrolment records. It sends the account notifications those businesses set up, from its own number and under its own brand. Each business has its own name, which it sets and can change at any time.

Every message opens by naming the business it concerns and Trainer Pro, so a parent always knows who it is from and what it is about. The form is always:

[Business Name] (via Trainer Pro): [message] Reply STOP to stop.

Filled in, for two different businesses using Trainer Pro:

Leah's Little Ones (via Trainer Pro): Hi Malky! Friendly reminder: the balance for Rivky and Moishy is $240. Thank you! Reply STOP to stop.
Sunny Days Playgroup (via Trainer Pro): Hi Chani! Received $150, thank you! The balance for Shaindy is now $0. Reply STOP to stop.

These messages go only to parents already enrolled with that business — people who handed over their child and their phone number in person. This is not marketing and it is not third-party messaging: nothing is ever sent to advertise Trainer Pro, to advertise anyone else, or on behalf of any party the parent has no relationship with. No phone number is ever sold, rented, shared between businesses, or given to advertisers or data brokers. Consent is recorded for one business only and is never transferred to another.

The opt-in, exactly as the parent sees it

The business enters the child's details on an enrolment form. Near the bottom of that form, under the heading Text messages, sits this checkbox. It is unticked when the form opens and stays unticked unless the parent asks for it to be ticked:

Text me balance reminders and schedule updates. Optional — you'll get the same service either way. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop.

Above is the real control, reproduced here so it can be inspected without an account. It is disabled on this page because this is documentation, not the live form.

Step by step

  1. A parent enrols their child with the business in person and gives their phone number for childcare purposes.
  2. The business opens the enrolment form in Trainer Pro and fills in the child's details.
  3. The business asks the parent whether they would like balance reminders by text.
  4. Only if the parent says yes is the box above ticked. It is never pre-ticked, and the form saves perfectly well with it left alone.
  5. The saved consent is stored against that parent's record. Every message the system sends checks it first: a record without consent is skipped on every text-sending path.
  6. A parent may withdraw at any time by replying STOP, or by asking the business to untick the box.

Frequency, cost, STOP and HELP

  • Frequency: recurring; typically 1–4 messages per month, depending on the business's billing schedule and account activity.
  • Cost: Trainer Pro does not charge for these messages. Message and data rates may apply from your carrier.
  • To stop: reply STOP to any message. You are unsubscribed immediately, you receive one confirmation, and nothing further is sent.
  • For help: reply HELP, contact the business that sends you the messages, or email hello@trainerpro.coach.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers are used only to deliver the account notifications the parent agreed to receive from the specific business they are enrolled with. See the Privacy Policy for the full statement.
