SMS Program Terms

Program name
Trainer Pro Account Notifications.
Program description
A business using Trainer Pro (for example a babysitter, day camp or daycare) sends its own enrolled customers account notifications: balance reminders, payment receipts, and schedule announcements such as closures orpickup time changes. Messages may include a secure link to view a balance or pay online. This is not a marketing program; no promotional or advertising messages are sent.
How to opt in — and that it is optional
Opting in is entirely voluntary and is never a condition of enrolling a child, using the service, creating an account, or making a payment. When a parent enrols with a business, they may separately and optionally provide a mobile number and tick an unticked box that reads: "Text me balance reminders and schedule updates. Optional — you'll get the same service either way. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop." A parent who leaves the box unticked receives no text messages and is served exactly the same. Consent is recorded per business and is never transferred to another business.
Message frequency
Recurring; typically 1–4 messages per month, depending on your account activity and the business's billing schedule.
Cost
Trainer Pro does not charge you for these messages. Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier.
How to stop
Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe immediately; you will receive a confirmation and no further messages. You may rejoin by asking the business to opt you in again.
How to get help
Reply HELP to any message, contact the business that sends you the messages, or email hello@trainerpro.coach.
Carriers
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is best-effort and depends on your carrier and device.
Privacy
No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See the Privacy Policy.
